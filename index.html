FAQ

Is Iron4fun an official IRONMAN activity? +

No. It is an independent guided swim training session designed to help athletes prepare safely for race day.

Can international athletes join? +

Yes. Sessions welcome athletes from across Europe and beyond, as long as they meet the required swim level.

Do I need to be a Swim4fun member? +

No. Members receive a special rate, but external athletes are welcome.

Do I need a wetsuit? +

Usually yes, depending on the season and water temperature. Final guidance is shared before your session.

What level do I need? +

You should be comfortable swimming the chosen distance continuously in open water. The form helps us group athletes correctly.

Are places limited? +

Yes. Each session has a maximum group size to preserve safety and coaching quality.
